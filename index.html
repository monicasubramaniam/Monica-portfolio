About

I'm Monica, a B.Sc. Information Technology graduate who has spent the last three years working close to operational data.

At Wipro, I worked on healthcare-related client operations using SQL, Excel, Alteryx, Jira, HRO Workbench and HRX Assist. I validated datasets, reconciled information, monitored KPIs, prepared monthly reports, and worked with teams to trace discrepancies back to their root cause.

Before that, I spent about two years at Focus Edumatics as an online tutor. I tracked each student's performance KPIs, read session-to-session trends, and turned them into specific feedback.

Both roles taught me to question inconsistencies, find the pattern, and explain findings clearly. I'm now deepening my work in SQL, Python, Pandas, NumPy, Power BI and exploratory analysis, with Data Science as a longer-term direction.
